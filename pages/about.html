About Pantrymoon

Pantrymoon sells practical books for people who grow their own, buy in season and hate throwing good food away.

We started with one book because it answers the question we kept asking ourselves every summer: how do you freeze the harvest so it is still good in February? Freeze Fresh by Crystal Schmidt answers that, one fruit or vegetable at a time.
