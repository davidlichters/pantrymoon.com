Shipping

Free US shipping. Arrives in 6 to 10 business days.

Processing

Orders leave our warehouse in the US within 1 to 2 business days. You get a tracking link by email as soon as your order ships.

Delivery time

Delivery in the US takes 5 to 8 business days after your order ships, so 6 to 10 business days from your order. Your expected delivery date is shown before checkout.

Christmas

Order by December 9 for delivery before Christmas within the US.

Where we ship

All 50 US states. We do not ship outside the US at the moment.

Shipping cost

Free on every order. There is no minimum and nothing is added at checkout.

Lost or damaged parcels

If tracking stops moving for 7 days, or your book arrives damaged, email us with your order number and a photo. We send a new copy free of charge.

Digital bonuses

The Freezer Kit printables come by email right after your order.
